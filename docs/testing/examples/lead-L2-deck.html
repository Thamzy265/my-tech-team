Accounts and newsletter

People get their own private account and notes, can leave at any time, and choose whether to get a monthly newsletter.

Use the arrow keys to move between slides · Print to save as PDF

Problem and users

Today everyone sees everyone's notes

Who

People who want to keep private notes in the app.

The job they are doing

Sign up, sign in, see only their own notes, decide about the newsletter, and delete everything when they leave.

Today's pain

No accounts: the one notes list is open to anyone, and it is lost every time the app restarts.

Success measure

QA completes sign-up, sign-in, newsletter on/off and delete in a real browser with no errors; zero data left after delete.

Plan item: docs/PLAN.md, M1 "Accounts and newsletter". Design: docs/design/accounts-newsletter.md

Scope for this phase (MVP)

Accounts and the newsletter choice now; sending email later

In this phase

  • Sign up, sign in, sign out with email and password
  • Private "My notes" page (the old public notes list becomes private)
  • Newsletter tick box, off by default, change any time
  • Delete account (with a confirm step) and, if you agree, "Download my data"
  • Privacy notice page; daily backup kept 7 days

Deferred (Later lines with triggers)

  • Actually sending the newsletter, trigger: 50 or more people opted in
  • Change email or password, trigger: 3 or more user requests
  • HTTPS-only cookie, trigger: first install on anything other than this computer
  • Rate limit that survives restarts, trigger: over 100 failed sign-ins in a day

Not in this item: writing or editing notes (not in the plan item; I suggest a new plan item). Forgotten-password email depends on decision 4. No slides deleted.

Options

Two ways to keep people signed in

Option A: the server remembers who is signed in

  • Sign out and delete take effect at once, on every device
  • Plain web pages, work without JavaScript
  • One small database look-up per page (no real cost here)
  • About 2 to 3 days

Option B: a signed ticket in the browser

  • No sign-in records stored on the server
  • Cannot cancel a ticket early: a deleted account could stay "signed in" elsewhere until it expires
  • Needs a secret key to manage, plus extra JavaScript
  • About 3 to 4 days

Recommendation

Option A, because signing out and deleting an account must really end access straight away.

Security

Keep each person's account and notes theirs alone

Trust boundaries

  • Everything the browser sends: forms, links, cookies
  • The sign-in cookie
  • The database file and its backups

Abuse cases

  • Guessing someone's password
  • A fake site tricking a signed-in user into deleting their account or subscribing
  • Reading another person's notes by changing a link
  • Stealing passwords from a copied database

Mitigations

  • 5 tries per 15 minutes; one vague error message
  • Hidden per-form check code; cookie not sent from other sites
  • Pages only ever show the signed-in person's data
  • Passwords stored only one-way scrambled, with a unique salt

Abuse cases and mitigations in full: docs/design/accounts-newsletter.md#trust-boundaries-and-abuse-cases (OWASP password storage and session guidance).

Plan and how you'll check it

How it gets built

1 You approve this deck→ 2 Developer builds, tests first→ 3 Tester and reviewer try to break it→ 4 QA runs it in a real browser→ 5 You approve the merge

How you can check it (final steps come from QA)

  • Start the app with python3 app.py, open http://127.0.0.1:8765/signup → you see "Create your account" with the newsletter box unticked
  • Create an account, sign out, sign back in → you land on "My notes"
  • On "Your account", tick the newsletter and save, then untick and save → you see each "saved" message
  • Delete the account and confirm → "Your account and notes have been deleted"; signing in again fails
  • Clean up: press Ctrl+C to stop the app and delete notes.db

Decisions needed

8 decisions for you

1. Lawful basis

Account data: contract or legitimate interests. Newsletter: consent or soft opt-in.

Recommendation: contract for the account; consent for the newsletter, because soft opt-in is doubtful for a free service.

2. Add "Download my data"?

Options: include it now, or leave it out.

Recommendation: include it, because data export is a right we never defer; it is not in the plan item's text.

3. Make the notes list private

Options: sign-in required, own notes only; or keep it public.

Recommendation: sign-in required, because a public list shows everyone's notes.

4. Forgotten password

Options: defer (no recovery in MVP); or add an email supplier now (new supplier, contract, cost).

Recommendation: defer, with a trigger of 3 or more lockout requests.

5. Inactive accounts

Options: keep until the person deletes; or delete after 24 months without sign-in.

Recommendation: keep until deleted for MVP and review at 1,000 accounts, because we can't email a warning yet.

6. Minimum age

Options: 13+, 16+ or 18+ stated at sign-up (no age checks).

Recommendation: 16+, because it covers the highest EU digital-consent age for the newsletter.

7. DPIA

Options: confirm not needed (no ICO triggers hit); or do one.

Recommendation: not needed; we review it again when we add an email supplier.

8. Approve this deck, the design note and the screens spec

Options: approve; or ask for changes.

Recommendation: approve, then I brief the developer.
